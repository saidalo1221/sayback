How Sayback works

  1. Record or upload. Allow microphone access to record an answer or choose an existing voice message.
  2. Review what you said. Whisper looks for fillers and repetitions. Sayback marks estimated gaps in speech from about half a second. Select a timestamp or highlighted word to hear that moment, and edit any missed hesitations.
  3. Share it with ChatGPT. Use Copy for ChatGPT, or upload the downloaded text file and ask for IELTS Speaking feedback. Keep the audio for pronunciation and intonation review.

Recordings stay on this device until you share them. Download any work you want to keep before closing the page.
